Where LatticeWire is going

What has shipped in protocol 3, and what comes next. Items move up the timeline as they land.

Protocol 3Shipped

Quantum-safe, fast and hard to spot.

  • Hybrid post-quantum handshakeX25519 + ML-KEM-768 with an ML-DSA-65 signature, one round trip
  • Signed cipher choiceAES-256-GCM on AES hardware, XChaCha20-Poly1305 elsewhere
  • ~7.7 Gb/s per streamAbout 1.4× wireguard-go in the same test bed
  • IPv4 and IPv6Inside the tunnel with NAT66, and as the transport
  • Flood shieldWireGuard-style cookies under load
  • Phantom and GhostQUIC-shaped stealth with continuing connection IDs
  • Per-client stealth keysRevoke one device without touching the rest
  • TLS fallbackAutomatic switch to TLS on 443 when UDP is blocked
